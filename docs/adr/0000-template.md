# NNNN - Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Accepted (implementation scheduled …) |
  Superseded by ADR-MMMM (link the superseding ADR when this happens)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: technical constraints, project goals, deadlines, prior
decisions. One or two paragraphs. Link the audit finding (`F#`), decision (`D#`)
or control (`SEC-*`) that motivates this, if any.

## Decision

The chosen option, stated plainly. Include the alternatives that were seriously
considered and why they lost.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what this commits us to. Include
follow-up work this decision creates (with the phase it is scheduled in).
